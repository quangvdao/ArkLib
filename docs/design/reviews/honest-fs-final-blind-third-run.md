## Scope and possible vacuity

These are exact equalities of projected laws or event probabilities. They do not give a positive or unit completeness probability, a numerical lower bound, witness validity, knowledge soundness, or equality of adversary logs, verifier logs, hash caches, or query costs. If a guard or the acceptance predicate always fails, both `isSome` probabilities can be zero; the named completeness equality is then vacuous as a positive-completeness guarantee. The zero-round case still samples the global salt and yields the unit path, subject to `accepts`.
